# CourtSync design system

Apple's design language, applied to a volleyball scheduler. The canonical prose is
`DESIGN-apple.md`; the tokens are `src/app/globals.css`. This is the rendered check.

There is no second brand colour, and no status colour: status is always a word
(and a symbol), never a colour alone.

## Shape and elevation

Radii: `sm` 8 for utility chrome, `lg` 18 for cards, `full` for pills. One shadow
exists and it belongs to the schedule grid — the one artifact the product makes.

## Links and focus

A link inside running text is underlined; one that stands alone is not. Every
focusable element shows a 2px focus ring. Tables wider than a phone scroll inside a
focusable, labelled region.
